Can this PC be turned on remotely?

This PC is on Wi-Fi. Over Wi-Fi it can be turned on from Sleep, and on some PCs from Hibernate, but not after a full shut down. Use Sleep when you want to turn it on again from your phone, or plug in an Ethernet cable for wake after shut down.

Help

Set up turning this PC on from your phone
  1. Plug this PC into the router with an Ethernet cable if you can. On Wi-Fi it works too, but only from Sleep (see the check above).
  2. BIOS: turn on Wake-on-LAN and turn ErP off. The name differs by brand: Wake on LAN, Power On By PCI-E or Resume by LAN.
  3. MSI boards: Settings > Advanced > Wake Up Event Setup, set "Wake Up Event By" to OS. Leaving it on BIOS can make the PC turn itself back on after every shutdown.
  4. Device Manager > Network adapters > your Ethernet adapter > Advanced: "Wake on Magic Packet" Enabled, "Wake on pattern match" Disabled.
  5. Same adapter > Power Management: tick all three boxes.
  6. Control Panel > Power Options > Choose what the power buttons do: untick "Turn on fast start-up".
  7. On the phone: Add PC, then enter the pairing code shown under "Connect your phone".
Use it away from home
Schedules always run, wherever you are. To turn this PC on or control it from outside your home network, your router has to let the phone in. The phone app walks you through it and tests it: open this PC in the phone app, tap the gear icon, then Away from home.

The safest way to control the PC from outside is a VPN into your home network (for example WireGuard on your router, or Tailscale). That needs no changes here. New phones can only ever be paired from inside your network.
The PC turns itself back on
A few seconds after shutting down: the BIOS is letting any part inside the PC wake it. On MSI boards set "Wake Up Event By" to OS; on other boards look for a setting that hands wake control to the operating system.

Later on: the network card is waking for ordinary traffic. Check the section above shows "Only the wake signal can wake this PC".
